NearCart maps the local bazaar and connects discovery to action.

NearCart is a digital discovery and commerce layer for local bazaars. It maps nearby shops, services, street-food stalls and local specialties, lets you compare prices, read reviews, chat with shopkeepers, confirm availability, reserve products, and choose pickup or delivery.

Every business is discoverable without needing to maintain live inventory. Detailed pricing, chat and reservation are richer capabilities merchants can add progressively.
